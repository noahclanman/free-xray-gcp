# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps the project:

- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing on what is best for the people who use the lists;
- showing empathy towards other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- insulting, demeaning or discriminatory comments;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing someone's private information without explicit permission;
- spam, advertising unrelated services, or pushing paid VPNs as "fixes" in issues;
- anything that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions) and in the Telegram
channel, **[@xray_ehi_hub](https://t.me/xray_ehi_hub)**, when it relates to the project.

## Enforcement

Report abusive behaviour privately to the maintainer via
[@xray_ehi_hub](https://t.me/xray_ehi_hub) or a GitHub Security Advisory.

The maintainer will review and may remove comments, close issues, block accounts or ban people
from the channel. Consequences scale with severity and repetition:

1. a private warning;
2. removal of the offending content;
3. a temporary ban;
4. a permanent ban.

Reports are handled confidentially. Good-faith reports will never be punished, even if the
conclusion is that no violation occurred.
